Microbe Crush Galaxy 1.0

**Microbe Crush Galaxy 1.0** is an interactive science-based match-3 game developed by **Dr AI Academy** for Class 8 students. In this game, students enter a sci-fi Microbe Galaxy where they match science tiles, complete missions, and learn about useful and harmful microorganisms. The game covers key concepts from **NCERT Class 8 Science — Microorganisms: Friend and Foe**, including yeast fermentation, curd formation, Rhizobium and nitrogen fixation, pathogens, vaccines, hygiene, and food safety. With Dr AI as a live guide, students receive voice motivation, concept explanations, and mission feedback while playing. The game is designed to move students away from rote learning and help them understand science through fun, challenge, and discovery.
