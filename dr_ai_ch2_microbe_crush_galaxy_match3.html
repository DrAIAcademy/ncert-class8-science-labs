The Story

Astra, the young science cadet, is flying through the Microbe Galaxy with Dr AI. The villain Lord Pathogen is spreading confusion — making students think all microbes are harmful. To restore balance, Astra must match science tiles and complete 3 galaxy missions: helpful microbes, defense against harmful microbes, and final mastery of the chapter.

How to Play

1
Tap one tile, then tap an adjacent tile to swap.
2
Make a line of 3 or more matching tiles.
3
Complete the mission targets before moves finish.
4
Each level teaches what students learned from the chapter.
