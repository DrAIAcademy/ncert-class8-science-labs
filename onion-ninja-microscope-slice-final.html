📘 About This Game

Onion Ninja Pro: Microscope Slice is an action-based science game where students play like a science ninja. They slice clean onion peels, avoid dust and bubbles, focus the microscope, identify onion cell parts, and complete a final germ challenge. The game connects arcade action with real classroom science so students learn by doing, not only by reading.

🎓 Educational Objective

The purpose of this game is to make the onion peel microscope experiment easy, visual and memorable. Students repeatedly connect each game action with a real science concept: clean specimen, thin peel, clear slide, focused microscope and visible plant cell parts.

🎯 What is this game about?

This game is based on the onion peel microscope activity. Students learn how onion peel is prepared, why the peel should be thin, how a microscope helps us see cells, and what important parts of an onion cell look like.

🧠 What students will learn

Thin onion peel: A thin peel allows light to pass through, so cells can be seen clearly.
Slide preparation: Dust, folds and air bubbles make the microscope view unclear.
Microscope focus: A microscope helps us observe tiny cells that cannot be seen clearly with naked eyes.
Onion cell parts: Students identify the cell wall, nucleus and vacuole.
Plant cell shape: The cell wall gives onion cells a fixed box-like shape.

🎮 How to play

  • Move left or right lane and slice only the correct objects.
  • Slice onion peels, cell parts and energy cards.
  • Avoid dust, air bubbles and germs.
  • Complete the target score of each stage.
  • Answer the science checkpoint question after every stage.
  • Complete all stages to unlock the certificate.

🕹️ Controls

Desktop: Left/Right Arrow or A/D to move. Space/Up Arrow to slice or action.

Mobile: Tap/swipe on the game area or use the bottom lane/action buttons.

🏆 Game stages

  • Stage 1: Peel Slice — slice clean onion peels and avoid dust.
  • Stage 2: Slide Dash — collect peels and avoid air bubbles.
  • Stage 3: Focus Ninja — hit the green focus zone.
  • Stage 4: Cell Slice — identify nucleus, cell wall and vacuole.
  • Stage 5: Germ Boss — finish the final microscope challenge.
