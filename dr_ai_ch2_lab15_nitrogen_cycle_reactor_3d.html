Concept Panel

Main Concept: Nitrogen moves between atmosphere, soil, microorganisms and plants.

Cycle Logic:
• Nitrogen fixation makes atmospheric nitrogen usable
• Decomposers recycle nitrogen back to soil
• Plants absorb usable nitrogen compounds

Why this matters: The nitrogen cycle supports growth, proteins, DNA and overall ecosystem balance.
