Concept Panel

Main Concept: Food poisoning can happen when harmful microbes or toxins enter food and then enter the body.

Prevention Logic:
• Proper cooking kills many microbes
• Cooling slows microbial growth
• Clean hands, utensils and water reduce contamination

Why this matters: This connects daily hygiene with public health and microbiology.
