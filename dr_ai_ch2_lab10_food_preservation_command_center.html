Concept Panel

Main Concept: Microbes need suitable temperature, moisture and nutrients to grow.

Preservation Logic:
• Cooling slows microbial activity
• Drying removes water
• Salt and sugar reduce water available to microbes

Why this matters: This is applied biology used in homes, industries, and food science.
