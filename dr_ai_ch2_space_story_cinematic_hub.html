Opening Space Crawl

In the far reaches of Microbe Galaxy, countless invisible organisms shape the fate of worlds. Some build life, heal planets and enrich soil. Others spread disease, spoil food and create chaos. The evil Lord Pathogen wants all students to fear science and stay trapped in ignorance. But hope rises from the star-ship Curiosity One. Guided by Dr AI, the young cadet hero Astra begins a mission to uncover the truth: microbes can be friends, microbes can be foes, and knowledge is the ultimate shield. From the Lens Planet to the Nitrogen Nebula, from the Fermentation Forge to the Vaccine Dome, the fate of learning now depends on one brave explorer...

Story Vision

Hero: Astra — a curious student space cadet.

Guide: Dr AI — mentor, scientist and mission commander.

Villain: Lord Pathogen — master of fear, contamination and confusion.

Main Theme: Science is not rote learning. It is discovery, protection, reasoning and action.
