Simple Rule

A question asks what you want to know. A hypothesis gives a testable explanation. A variable is what you change. A control means other conditions stay same. A prediction tells what you expect to happen if your hypothesis is correct.

Level 4: Olympiad Reasoning Quiz

Now show that you understand hypothesis building, not just option selection.

Q1. Which statement is the best scientific hypothesis?
If oil temperature is too low, then puri puffing will become weak because steam forms more slowly.
Puri is nice and interesting.
Maybe something happens somehow.
Q2. Why must a hypothesis be testable?
Because we should be able to check it by observation or experiment.
Because it should sound difficult.
Because it should be impossible to test.
Q3. Which skill is most useful for JEE / NEET / Olympiad science investigations?
Asking a good question, testing one variable, and using evidence to conclude.
Guessing quickly without checking.
Changing many things together and hoping for the best.
