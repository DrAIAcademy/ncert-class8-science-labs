Concept Summary

What happened?
Rhizobium bacteria live in root nodules of legumes.

Why useful?
They convert atmospheric nitrogen into useful nitrogen compounds.

Big Idea:
This is symbiosis. Plant gives shelter/food; bacteria help plant with nitrogen.
